## 2. About Advertorial Hub

Advertorial Hub is a Lagos-based advertisement and marketing firm dedicated to helping individuals and businesses create compelling, high-quality content for public consumption. Our services include:

- Producing and managing content strategies for client products and brands
- Creating internal digital storefronts for businesses with little to no technical experience
- Acting as a full-service promotional outsourcing agency for clients across various industries
- Leveraging artificial intelligence and modern content tools to deliver results-driven, scalable media

The Employee is engaged as a Creator and Content Assistant to produce content, manage pseudo accounts, build stores, and drive real-world adoption of the Advertorial Hub platform.

## 3. Job Description

### 3.1 Position

**Creator & Content Assistant**

### 3.2 Employment Type

Full-time, remote. The Employee may engage with other employers or clients simultaneously, provided such engagements do not conflict with Advertorial Hub or violate this Agreement.

### 3.3 Reporting Line

The Employee shall report directly to the Lead Developer of Advertorial Hub. All task assignments, feedback, and communications shall flow through this reporting structure unless otherwise stated in writing.

The Employee shall work alongside **Eko Mmesoma Patience (Imo State)**, supporting her content execution, posting on Facebook and Twitter, and outreach tasks.

### 3.4 Pseudo Accounts

The Employee shall maintain three (3) pseudo accounts on the Advertorial Hub website, each assigned a specific niche. These accounts are for publishing articles, testing content strategies, and building audience presence within each niche.

### 3.5 Core Responsibilities

The Employee shall be responsible for, but not limited to, the following:

- Creating a minimum of three (3) videos per week promoting Advertorial Hub services, platform features, or niche-specific content aligned with pseudo accounts
- Writing and publishing a minimum of three (3) articles per week across the three pseudo accounts on the Advertorial Hub website (three article per account per week minimum)
- Setting up and maintaining one (1) store for each pseudo account on the Advertorial Hub platform with a minimum of three (2) products listed per month
- Experimenting with organic and low-cost strategies to grow product visibility and sales in real-world conditions, documenting what works and what does not
- Managing content posting and engagement on Advertorial Hub's Twitter (X), and Facebook accounts
- Identifying high-potential content, products, or videos and flagging them to management for potential ad spend
- Attending required training sessions and working with proprietary tools introduced by Advertorial Hub
- Completing all assigned content tasks within stated deadlines and responding promptly to feedback and revision requests

### 3.6 Physical Assignments

The Employee may be sent on physical assignments to meet store owners and potential platform users in person, not exceeding two (2) assignments per month. Miscellaneous expenses (transport, feeding, communication) incurred during these assignments shall be provided or reimbursed by the company.

### 3.7 Tour Participation

If Advertorial Hub organizes a state tour within Lagos State, the Employee is expected to participate in all scheduled activities, including market visits, user onboarding sessions, and content production during the tour.

### 3.8 Team Coordination

The Employee shall coordinate daily with **Eko (Imo State)** to align on content schedules, posting cadence, and task priorities across Facebook and Twitter.

### 3.9 Minimum Output Requirement

- 3 videos per week (minimum 12 per 30-day cycle)
- 3 articles per week each across 3 pseudo accounts (minimum 12 per 30-day cycle)
- 1 store with 3 products listed for each user per month (minimum 3 products added per 30-day cycle)

Failure to consistently meet these minimums without prior written notice and approval may be treated as a performance concern.

## 5. Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

### 5.1 Purpose

This section constitutes a binding Non-Disclosure Agreement between Advertorial Hub (“the Disclosing Party”) and Gaji Yaqub (“the Receiving Party”). Its purpose is to protect the proprietary strategies, business information, processes, and intellectual assets of Advertorial Hub that the Employee will be exposed to during this engagement.

### 5.2 Definition of Confidential Information

For the purposes of this Agreement, “Confidential Information” includes, but is not limited to:

- all marketing strategies, campaign plans, content strategies, and go-to-market approaches developed, discussed, or used by Advertorial Hub
- client information, including names, products, briefs, data, and materials shared in confidence
- proprietary tools, platforms, systems, or software built or commissioned by Advertorial Hub
- business models, pricing structures, revenue information, and internal financial data
- workflow processes, operating procedures, and internal communications
- any information shared during training sessions, internal meetings, or task briefings that is not publicly available

### 5.3 Obligations of the Employee

During the term of this Agreement and for a period of forty-five (45) days following its conclusion, the Employee agrees to:

- hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent from Advertorial Hub
- not reproduce, publish, share, or make available any Confidential Information in any form (digitally, verbally, or in writing)
- use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of fulfilling duties under this Agreement
- promptly notify Advertorial Hub if the Employee becomes aware of any unauthorised disclosure or potential breach
- return or permanently delete any Confidential Information in their possession upon request or upon conclusion of this Agreement

### 5.4 Exclusions

The obligations in Section 5.3 do not apply to information that:

- was already in the public domain at the time of disclosure, through no fault of the Employee
- was independently known to the Employee prior to its disclosure under this Agreement
- is required to be disclosed by law, court order, or government authority, provided that Advertorial Hub receives prompt prior written notice

### 5.5 Duration of Confidentiality Obligations

The Employee's confidentiality obligations begin on the date this Agreement is signed and remain in full force throughout the engagement and for forty-five (45) days after its conclusion or termination, regardless of the reason for termination.

### 5.6 Consequences of Breach

Any breach of this Non-Disclosure Agreement may cause significant and irreparable harm to Advertorial Hub. In the event of a breach or threatened breach, Advertorial Hub reserves the right to pursue all available legal remedies, including damages and injunctive relief. The Employee acknowledges that monetary compensation alone may not be a sufficient remedy.
