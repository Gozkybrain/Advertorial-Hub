# Advertorial Hub — Activity Map

**Pre-Launch Start:** June 20  
**Launch Date:** July 20  
**6-Month Horizon:** July 20 → January 20  
**Targets:** 1k members at launch → 5k total by December. 20k posts, 2k stores, 20k products on the platform.

## Phase 1: Pre-Launch (June 20 → July 19)

### Goal
Build waitlist, onboard early creators and store owners, gather content inventory through creator team.


### Creator Team Setup

| Lagos | 3 creators |

| Face videos per creator per week | 3 |
| Graphics per creator per week | 4 |
| **Total weekly output** | **21 face videos + 28 graphics** |

### Creator Content Pipeline

| Activity | Strategy | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Brief creators | Teach them the Advertorial Hub value prop — how market vendors and small businesses can sell online through advertorials. | All 7 creators aligned |
| Face videos (3/creator/week) | Product demos, store walkthroughs, market testimonial-style clips, "why digital selling matters" | 21 videos/week |
| Graphics (4/creator/week) | Promotional graphics for stores, product highlights, social shareables | 28 graphics/week |
| Content distribution | Creators post on their own channels + Advertorial Hub reposts. Tag market partners. | Compounding reach |

### Join Promo — 2 Months Free Starter Plan

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Offer | All new users get 2 months free on the Starter plan (₦5,000 value) |
| Claim limit | Once per user |

**Pre-Launch Flow (June 20 → July 19)**
- Users join the waitlist
- They must wait until launch day (July 20) to claim their 2-month free starter plan
- Claim opens when the platform goes live

**Post-Launch Flow (July 21 onward)**
- New users register on the platform
- They receive an email prompting them to claim their 2-month free starter plan
- One claim per user, same as pre-launch users

### Goal

| Activity | Strategy |

## Phase 3: Post-Launch (July 21 → December)

### Goal
Compound traffic. Grow to 5k members, 20k posts, 2k stores, 20k products.

### Creator Team Output (Ongoing)

| Metric | Weekly | Monthly | 6 Months |
|---|---|---|---|
| Face videos | 21 | 84 | ~500 |
| Graphics | 28 | 112 | ~670 |
| **Total content pieces** | **49** | **196** | **~1,170** |

### Awareness Strategy

| Activity | Cadence | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Guest on Sarcastic Geeks Spaces | 1–2x per week | Continue appearing as speakers. Rotate topics: store spotlights, market deep dives, success stories. |
| Creator content drops | Daily | 7 creators posting consistently = daily presence across multiple channels. |
| Market & campus tours | Per schedule | Joint tours with Hello World. Creator team documents and produces content from each visit. |

### Market & Campus Tours (Joint with Hello World)

| Activity | Strategy | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Visit Alaba, Ladipo, major markets across 5 states | Run physical onboarding. Help traders set up stores and list products on the spot. Creator team films testimonial videos. | 500+ new stores |
| Visit universities (Imo, Anambra, Enugu, Abia, Lagos) | Joint session with Hello World — Hello World covers job-seeking, Advertorial Hub covers digital selling & content marketing. | 1,000+ new members |
| Content from tours | Creator team turns each visit into videos + graphics. Feeds the weekly content pipeline. | 20+ episodes |

### Compounding Traffic Strategy

| Activity | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Store owners invite buyers | Each store owner shares their store link → buyers see the platform → some sign up as sellers. |
| Product shares go viral | Each product listed is a shareable advertorial → drives traffic back. |
| 20k products = 20k entry points | Every product is a potential first touchpoint for a new user. |
| Market associations as multipliers | Onboard one market leader → they bring their entire network. |
| Creator content = constant top-of-funnel | 49 pieces of content per week = daily discovery for new audiences. |
